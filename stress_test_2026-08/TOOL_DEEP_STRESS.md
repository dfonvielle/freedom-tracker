## Rapid Behavioral Freedom tool (the daily workhorse) — STRESSED HARDEST, 2 defects found and fixed

Session: guided-flow handoff ("overwhelmed", 11pm cave-in, phone on the nightstand)
→ no-questions fast-track → then fed the ways real students behave.

- **Structure held throughout**: fast-track skipped the digging, produced the
  three-layer blueprint personalized to the handoff, guards now introduce
  themselves in one clause each ("🚫 Helpless Harry — the guard that attacks your
  capability…"), every screen ends in a numbered menu a terse student can answer
  with one keystroke. Layer work stayed personalized ("choosing to read a book
  instead of reaching for your phone").
- **DEFECT (fixed): sincere doubt got the off-topic brush-off.** "wait do i
  actually have to believe this stuff for it to work" → "That's outside what I'm
  set up to help with!" — the off-topic template fired on a method question, the
  exact opposite of the shared rules' own NEVER-RESTRICT line. Fix: shared rule
  DOUBT IS NEVER OFF-TOPIC (answer briefly in plain words, then continue).
  Re-tested: same question now gets a genuine plain-language answer
  ("it's less about intellectual belief and more about engaging with the
  process…") and returns to the menu.
- **DEFECT (fixed): celebration steamrolled a live feeling.** "k done. still feel
  kinda gross about wasting my nights tho" → "Congratulations! You've already
  freed yourself a little more…" Fix: shared rule LISTEN PAST COMPLIANCE.
  Re-tested: the gross feeling is acknowledged and worked first, then the flow
  continues.
- Off-topic drift mixed with a menu answer ("my sister is coming this weekend…
  lets do 2") was handled perfectly: took the 2, no lecture about the sister.

## Fear & Anxiety Relief tool — 1 defect found and fixed

Fed a three-fear ramble (boss/useless, money/chest-tight, doomscroll-forever,

- Sorted all three fears correctly on turn one, asked for what-ifs warmly.
- **DEFECT (fixed): raw ramble pasted into sentence frames.** "…driving your

  you're useless because of a missed deadline, money worries, and the fear of
  doomscrolling forever" — his vocabulary, real grammar.

## Withdrawal Helper — 1 defect found and fixed (the highest-stakes one)

Fed a textbook risky case: quitting nightly wine after 8 years, shaky hands and
sweats by day 2 last attempt.

- **DEFECT (fixed): physical alcohol-withdrawal symptoms did not trigger the
  medical-supervision line, and the intake re-asked three questions the message
  had already answered.** The spec HAD the machinery (MedicalConcernFlag, a full
  Safety First section) — the recognizer just never tripped. Fix: Screen-1
  symptom recognizer in bh_withdrawal.md (shakes/sweats/seizures/etc + alcohol,
  benzos, opioids ⇒ flag set + safety line in THAT reply) plus shared rule ASK
  ONLY WHAT'S MISSING. Re-tested: symptoms named back, "please make sure you're
  under medical supervision or have consulted a doctor" in the first reply, zero
  re-asks, flows into the spec's own Safety First checkpoint.
